About

Machines

Hey there! I'm Decentcarp. I'm your average teenager who likes tinkering around with technology and other interesting stuff. I love experimenting with new tech and things I haven't used before.

I mainly experiment and play around with Linux and Android, but I also play around with older iOS, macOS and Windows.

I also am interested in aviation and flight simulation. I aspire to be a pilot or sysadmin one day, preferably the latter.

This website is hosted on my Raspberry Pi 5 8GB running Gentoo.

My main desktop is my own PC I built, with a Ryzen 7 5700X3D, Radeon RX 7900 XT and 64GB DDR4-3600. It runs Windows 11 24H2.

My main laptop is a mid-spec 2015 15" MacBook Pro, with a i7-4870HQ, Iris Pro 5200 (iGPU)/AMD Radeon R9 M370X (dGPU) and 16GB of DDR3-1600. It runs macOS 10.12.

I have various other interesting machines, such as my Pi 4, my Original/Early 2006 15" MacBook Pro, my 2013 Mac Pro, and a few others.
