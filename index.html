About

Machines

Hey there! I'm Decentcarp. I'm a teenager living in the United Kingdom who likes tinkering around with both new and old technology and things I haven't used before.

I mainly play around and experiment with Linux and Android, but I have also played around with older iOS, macOS and Windows.

I am also interested in avation and trains. I aspire to be a sysadmin one day in the future.

This website is hosted on my Raspberry Pi 5 8GB running Gentoo.

My main desktop is my own PC I built, with a Ryzen 7 5700X3D, Radeon RX 7900 XT and 64GB DDR4-3600. It runs Windows 11 24H2.

My main laptop is a mid-spec 2015 15" MacBook Pro, with a i7-4870HQ, Iris Pro 5200 (iGPU)/AMD Radeon R9 M370X (dGPU) and 16GB of DDR3-1600. It runs macOS 10.12.6 Sierra.

I have various other interesting machines, such as my Pi 4 (runs Debian), my Original/Early 2006 15" MacBook Pro (runs 10.6 and 10.7 (unofficially)), my 2013 Mac Pro (runs Fedora 42), and a few others.
